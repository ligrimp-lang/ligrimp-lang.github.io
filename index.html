Five iPhone apps for divers.

Gas planning, decompression, weighting, your Shearwater's log and your dive videos. Built by a diver. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.

Turn Pressure

Scuba gas & no-deco calculator

Recreational dive planner: turn pressure, minimum gas and bottom time for the dive you're actually doing — not a flat 50 bar. Your own SAC rate, repeat dives, a dive log. Free, offline, no account.

Download on the App Store Details, support & privacy

Time to Surface

Trimix & nitrox decompression planner

Technical dive and gas planner for up to 56 metres: six bottom gases, one deco bottle, Bühlmann ZH-L16C with your own gradient factors, up to three dives before this one. Free, offline, no account.

Download on the App Store Details, support & privacy

Weights Check

Scuba buoyancy calculator

Your rig, your lead, your water — a library instead of memory. The balance drawn at depth, estimates until you record a weight check, then your own numbers. Free, offline, no account.

Download on the App Store Details, support & privacy

Surface Interval

Underwater video colour and dive reels

Add a dive's videos and the app does the rest: colour restored, the best moments trimmed and put in order, music under them. Nothing is uploaded. Free for two reels a day, on iPhone and Mac.

Download on the App Store Details, support & privacy

Dive log

Shearwater dive import

Every dive from your Shearwater, drawn in full: depth, temperature, stops, gas switches and time to surface, as the computer recorded them. Over Bluetooth, no cable. Free, private, no account.

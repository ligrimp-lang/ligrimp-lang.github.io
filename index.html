Apps for divers, today and every day.

These apps help with gas planning, decompression, weighting, your Shearwater's log and your dive videos, on iPhone and Mac. A diver built them. They need no account, and they have no ads and no tracking. You enter your data once, and every app knows it through your own iCloud.

Answers for divers

These short episodes explain gas planning and weighting in plain words. Each one answers one question and finds the answer step by step on one example. Each episode has its full transcript on its own page.

Surface consumption rate (SCR)

Calculate your SCR, also called SAC, from any dive, step by step, in about two minutes.

How much gas do you need for the dive?

Calculate the gas for a dive with C × A × T, step by step, in about two and a half minutes. Your tank pressure must cover the gas for the dive plus minimum gas.

Minimum gas

Calculate the gas two divers need to ascend from the deepest point on one tank, step by step, in about three minutes.

Balanced rig

Find the lead you need, how heavy the rig is at the start, and where the lead goes for trim, step by step, in under two minutes.

Diver.Today

Your iPhone dive apps on the Mac, with the same data

Everything your iPhone apps do, on the Mac, in one window. Diver.Today covers the features of Dive log, Turn Pressure, Time to Surface and Weights Check, and it uses the same data as they do. Change a dive, a buddy, a rig or a plan on your Mac and it appears on your iPhone and in the other apps in real time, and the other way round.

  • Shearwater dive log. Dive log’s dives, statistics, search, buddies and sites, downloaded over Bluetooth.
  • Recreational Planner. Turn Pressure’s turn pressure, minimum gas and briefs.
  • Technical Planner. Time to Surface’s decompression schedule, gas plan and signed team plans.
  • My equipment and Balance rig. Weights Check’s gear, rigs, balance and weight checks.

Time to Surface

Trimix deco and technical dive planner

A decompression planner for dives up to 56 metres. It covers a swim to the drop-off, two deep segments, six bottom gases, one deco bottle, Bühlmann ZH-L16C with your own gradient factors, and up to three dives before this one. You can send the plan as a picture or a PDF. It is free, works offline and needs no account.

Time to Surface for dive pros

Signed team dive plans, in your dive centre’s brand

Plan the dive, send it for signatures, and hand the dive centre one page the whole team has signed: the schedule, the gases, the gas plan and each diver’s signature, as a picture or a one-page A4 PDF. The app keeps every plan by its date, and you can search across sites and teams. With the Diver.Today App Family subscription, your logo, your pictures and your colour appear on the app and on every page it sends. The subscription is free for a week, then you pay monthly, yearly or once for life, and one subscription covers every Diver.Today app. The planner and briefs stay free.

Surface Interval

Underwater video colour correction & reels

Fix blue GoPro dive footage and turn it into one reel. Add a dive's videos, and the app restores the colour, trims the best moments, puts them in order and adds music under them. It is the same app on iPhone and on Mac, with the same folders and reels. It is free for two reels a day.

Dive log

Shearwater dive log app for iPhone

Import your Shearwater dives once, over Bluetooth or from Shearwater Cloud, into a dive logbook. Each dive is on one page, and navigation is super fast. A backup computer’s copy of a dive is merged automatically. You can search your tags and buddies, see your surface consumption rate (SCR) for each dive and over time, see every site on one map, and make a card for social media. Your name, your picture and your colour appear on the app and on the card. It is free, private and needs no account.

Turn Pressure

Dive planner and minimum gas calculator

A recreational scuba gas planner and no-deco calculator. It calculates turn pressure, minimum gas and bottom time from your depth, your cylinder and your gas consumption. It uses your own surface consumption rate (SCR), handles repeat dives and keeps a dive log. Your divers and your saved briefs are in your own iCloud and shared with Time to Surface. It is free, works offline and needs no account.

Weights Check

Scuba weight and buoyancy calculator

Weights Check helps you calculate buoyancy for your equipment and keep track of your weights and configurations. Record a weight check and the calculator adjusts to your own numbers. Your rigs, gear and checks are in your own iCloud, and the dives you link in Dive log are listed on each rig’s page*. It is free, works offline and needs no account.

One iCloud, six apps

All your data is in your own iCloud, on every iPhone and Mac you sign in to and in every Diver.Today app, in real time. You enter it once.

  • Divers and briefs. The divers you add in Turn Pressure, with their breathing rates, and the briefs you save are there in Time to Surface, and the other way round.
  • Rigs and dives. You pick the rig you built in Weights Check in Dive log, and the dives you link are listed on the rig’s page.*
  • Buddies and dive sites. You name them once in Time to Surface, and they are there in every app.
  • Your look, one subscription. The Diver.Today App Family subscription is launching now. It puts your name, logo, pictures and colour on every app and on everything it exports. You set them once, and iCloud carries them to the other apps. One subscription covers all five apps. Time to Surface has it first, and Dive log comes next.

* Arrives with Dive log, now in Apple review. The other four apps and their iCloud sharing are on the App Store now.

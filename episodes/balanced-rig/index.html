Balanced rig, step by step

Rustam Ismailov, 2 November 2026 · an episode of under two minutes

Too much lead or too little makes every dive harder. A rig is balanced when it passes two checks, one at the end of the dive and one at the start.

The two checks

At the endAt the 3 m stop, with a nearly empty tank and an empty wing, you stay without effort.
At the startAt the deepest point, with a full tank, you can ascend by swimming if the wing fails.

Our example

WaterRed Sea
Suit5 mm wetsuit
Tankone aluminium tank, 11.1 L
Wing30 lb, about 13.6 kg of lift
Deepest point30 m
Full tank200 bar

Step by step

  1. The lead you need

    3 m stop · 50 bar · empty wing → 4.0 kg of lead

    Change the lead until you stay at the stop without effort. With 4.0 kg, our rig held neutral.

  2. The rig at the start

    30 m · 200 bar · empty wing → 4.6 kg heavy

    Heavy means how much the rig pulls you down with an empty wing. Compared with the stop, the full tank has about 1.9 kg more air, and the compressed wetsuit has lost about 2.6 kg of lift. Most divers can ascend by swimming with a rig about 5 kg heavy, so our rig is close to that limit. Test your own.

  3. Where the lead goes

    feet or head sink → move the lead → horizontal

    Where the lead sits changes your trim, the angle of your body. Move the lead and the heavy items until you stay horizontal, with your hands and fins still.

    Trim: what you see, and the cause
    In the waterThe cause
    The feet sinkThe lead sits too far towards the feet.
    The head sinksThe lead sits too far towards the head.
    You stay horizontalThe lead is in the right place.

The rule

At the start, you must be able to ascend by swimming if the wing fails. Most divers can do it with a rig about 5 kg heavy, but test your own limit. If you cannot, change the gear or carry part of the lead where you can drop it. A steel tank, a thick wetsuit and too much lead all make the rig heavier at the start.

A rig is balanced when it passes two checks. At the end, you stay at the last stop without effort. At the start, you can ascend by swimming if the wing fails.

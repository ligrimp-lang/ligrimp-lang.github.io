What is your SAC rate?

Rustam Ismailov, 29 October 2026 · an episode of under a minute

Your SAC rate is what you breathe in a minute at the surface, and every gas plan starts from it. It is the C in C × A × T, the cat that gives the gas a dive needs. You do not guess it: you take it from a dive you have done, and this episode shows how, in under a minute.

What is said

Every gas plan starts with your SAC rate: what you breathe in a minute, at the surface. You get it from a dive you have done.

Three things from that dive. The gas you used: say a hundred and twenty bar, from a twelve-litre cylinder. How long: forty minutes. And the average depth your computer recorded: fourteen metres.

A hundred and twenty bar times twelve litres: fourteen hundred and forty litres. Over forty minutes, thirty-six litres a minute. At fourteen metres the pressure was two point four bar, so you breathed two point four times the gas. Divide by two point four, and your SAC rate is fifteen litres a minute.

Do it for a few dives and use the average. Dive log works it out from each dive's profile and pressures. Free on the App Store. diver.today.

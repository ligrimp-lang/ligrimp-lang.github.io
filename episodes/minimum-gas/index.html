What is minimum gas?

Rustam Ismailov, 29 October 2026 · an episode of under a minute

Minimum gas is what must still be in your cylinder so that two divers can get up from the deepest point when one of them has no gas. It is the same cat, C × A × T, for two stressed divers on the way up. This episode works it out once, for a made-up dive, in under a minute; the written answer, with the method step by step, is on the minimum gas page.

What is said

At the deepest point your buddy signals: out of gas. You share, and you both go up. How much must be left for that? That is minimum gas.

Work it out with the cat: consumption times pressure times time, for two on the way up. C: two stressed divers, say twenty litres a minute each. Forty together.

A minute at thirty metres to sort it out. Thirty metres is four bar: a hundred and sixty litres. Then the ascent at nine metres a minute: three point three minutes at two and a half bar on average, three hundred and thirty litres.

Nearly five hundred litres: from an eleven-litre cylinder, forty-five bar. When your gauge gets there, the dive is over, whatever the plan said. Turn Pressure works it out for your dive. Free on the App Store. diver.today.

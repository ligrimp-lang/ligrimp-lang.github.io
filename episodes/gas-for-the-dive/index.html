How much gas do you need for the dive?

How much gas do you need for the dive, and is your tank fill enough? The gas a dive needs is easy to remember: a cat, C × A × T. What is said

How much gas do you need for the dive? You plan a dive to twenty metres for thirty-five minutes, with a tank filled to two hundred bar. Is that fill enough for the dive and a reserve? Let's calculate the answer before you dive.

The gas a dive needs is easy to remember: a cat, C times A times T. C is your consumption, the surface consumption rate, SCR. It is the litres you breathe per minute at the surface. A is the average pressure, in atmospheres. T is the time, in minutes.

For example, in our dive the average depth is twenty metres, which is also the deepest point, and the dive lasts thirty-five minutes. The tank holds eleven point one litres, and your SCR is fifteen litres per minute. Step one: let's calculate the average pressure, A. Twenty metres divided by ten, plus one, is three atmospheres. Step two: let's calculate the gas you breathe per minute at depth, C times A. Fifteen litres per minute times three atmospheres is forty-five litres per minute.

Step three: let's calculate the gas for the whole dive, times T. Forty-five litres per minute times thirty-five minutes is one thousand five hundred and seventy-five litres. Step four: let's calculate the same gas in bar, because your gauge shows bar. One thousand five hundred and seventy-five litres divided by the tank's volume, eleven point one litres, is one hundred and forty-two bar.

Now let's calculate the total: the gas for the dive plus minimum gas. Minimum gas is the gas two divers need to ascend from the deepest point, and for twenty metres it is sixty bar. One hundred and forty-two bar plus sixty bar is two hundred and two bar. That is more than the two hundred bar fill. The rule: your tank pressure must cover the gas for the dive plus minimum gas. This fill is not enough, so reduce the dive time, or take a bigger tank. How to calculate minimum gas is in another video on this channel.
